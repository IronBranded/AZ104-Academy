# Lab 00-01 - Budgets and Cost Alerts

**Objective:** (Project prerequisite - not an AZ-104 exam objective)
**Estimated cost:** $0 — a budget, an action group with one email receiver, and saved cost analysis views. Action
group email notifications are free up to 1,000 emails a month, far more than this lab sends.
**Licensing required:** None beyond an Azure subscription.

> Run this before you create anything billable. It's the one lab whose resources you **keep** for the whole course:
> `rg-az104-core`, the action group and the budget.

## Why this matters

Nobody else is watching your lab subscription's bill. Azure keeps charging for what exists, cost data arrives hours
late, and a pay-as-you-go subscription has no spending limit. This lab gives you an alarm before you need one: a
budget that emails you as spending heads past your ceiling, and a saved view that shows which lab the money went to.

## The desired state

When you finish, these will be true. Each one is checked in **Validation** below.

- A resource group `rg-az104-core` exists in your lab subscription.
- An **action group** `ag-az104-budget` in it is **enabled** and emails you, and a test notification has reached your
  inbox.
- A monthly **budget** `bud-az104-monthly` on the lab subscription has three alert conditions (Forecasted 80%,
  Actual 90%, Actual 100%), each sending to the action group.
- A saved **cost analysis** view groups the subscription's costs by the `az104-module` tag.

## Part 1 - The long-lived resource group and the action group

Do this part one way: portal, CLI or PowerShell.

### Portal

1. **Resource groups** > **Create**. Subscription: your lab subscription. Name `rg-az104-core`. Region: your home
   region. **Review + create** > **Create**.
2. **Monitor** > **Alerts** > **Action groups** > **Create**.
3. **Basics**: resource group `rg-az104-core`, region **Global**, action group name `ag-az104-budget`, display name
   `AZ104Lab`.
4. **Notifications**: type **Email/SMS message/Push/Voice**, name `me`, tick **Email**, enter your address, **OK**.
   Leave **Actions** empty.
5. **Review + create** > **Create**.
6. Check your inbox. A new email address gets a **one-time passcode** from Azure: enter it within 30 minutes (if it
   expires, open the action group and select **Resend**). Until it's verified, the address receives no alerts and no
   test notifications.

### Azure CLI

```bash
az group create --name rg-az104-core --location <home-region> --tags az104-module=00-01

az monitor action-group create \
  --name ag-az104-budget \
  --resource-group rg-az104-core \
  --location Global \
  --short-name AZ104Lab \
  --action email me <you@example.com>
```

The action group is created enabled. Then verify the email address with the one-time passcode Azure sends, as in
step 6 of the portal method.

### Azure PowerShell

```powershell
New-AzResourceGroup -Name 'rg-az104-core' -Location '<home-region>' -Tag @{ 'az104-module' = '00-01' }

$me = New-AzActionGroupEmailReceiverObject -Name 'me' -EmailAddress '<you@example.com>'
New-AzActionGroup -Name 'ag-az104-budget' -ResourceGroupName 'rg-az104-core' -Location 'Global' `
                  -GroupShortName 'AZ104Lab' -EmailReceiver $me -Enabled
```

Don't leave out `-Enabled`: without it, the action group is created disabled and sends nothing, with no error to tell
you. Then verify the email address with the one-time passcode Azure sends, as in step 6 of the portal method.

### What is happening?

The action group is a small Azure resource that holds a list of receivers. On its own it does nothing; anything that
alerts in Azure, a budget here and metric and log alerts in module 05, can call it. Putting it in `rg-az104-core`
keeps it out of every lab resource group you'll delete.

## Part 2 - The budget

Create the budget in the **portal**. Microsoft documents that budgets created with PowerShell don't send
notifications, and an alarm that doesn't ring is worse than none.

1. **Subscriptions** > your lab subscription > **Budgets** (under **Cost Management**) > **Add**.
2. Check the **Scope** shows the lab subscription. Leave the filters empty, so the budget covers every lab.
3. Name `bud-az104-monthly`. Reset period **Monthly**. Expiration date: a year or more out. Amount: your ceiling.
   **Next**.
4. Add three alert conditions, and choose `ag-az104-budget` as the action group for each:

   | Type | % of budget | Action group |
   | --- | --- | --- |
   | Forecasted | 80 | `ag-az104-budget` |
   | Actual | 90 | `ag-az104-budget` |
   | Actual | 100 | `ag-az104-budget` |

5. **Alert recipients (email)**: your address. **Create**.
6. Add `azure-noreply@microsoft.com`, `azureemail-noreply@microsoft.com` and `alerts-noreply@mail.windowsazure.com` to
   your email's safe senders list.

### What is happening?

Notice what the form let you do: nothing on it stops spending. A budget compares the subscription's cost data with
your amount about once a day. The forecasted condition warns you early, when the month's *trend* will cross 80%; the
actual conditions tell you when the money is already spent. The action group means the warning still reaches you if
you later change your email in one place rather than in every budget.

## Part 3 - Test the action group

An alarm you haven't tested is an alarm you're assuming works.

1. **Monitor** > **Alerts** > **Action groups** > `ag-az104-budget` > **Test**.
2. Sample type: **Budget**. Select the email notification `me`. **Test**, and wait for the result on the page.
3. Check your inbox (and junk folder) for the test email. No email usually means the address hasn't been verified yet
   (Part 1) or a safe-senders rule is missing (Part 2).

From the CLI, the same test is `az monitor action-group test-notifications create --action-group ag-az104-budget
--resource-group rg-az104-core --alert-type budget --add-action email me <you@example.com>`.

---

## Part 4 - A cost view you check every session

1. **Cost Management** > **Cost analysis**. Set the scope to the lab subscription.
2. Open a customizable view such as **Daily costs**. **Group by**: **Tag** > `az104-module`. Date range: this month.
3. **Save as** `az104 lab costs`.

Today the view will be empty or show only *Untagged*: nothing billable exists yet, and tags appear in cost data only
for usage reported after the tag was applied. From the next lab on, each lab's costs appear under its own module id.
Resource group tags aren't used in this grouping, which is why every lab tags its resources directly.

## Validation

```powershell
# The action group exists, is enabled, and emails you
Get-AzActionGroup -ResourceGroupName 'rg-az104-core' -Name 'ag-az104-budget' |
    Select-Object Name, Enabled, GroupShortName, @{ n = 'Emails'; e = { $_.EmailReceiver.EmailAddress } }
```

```bash
# The same check from the CLI, and the budget's alert conditions
az monitor action-group show -n ag-az104-budget -g rg-az104-core \
  --query "{name:name, enabled:enabled, emails:emailReceivers[].emailAddress}"
az consumption budget show --budget-name bud-az104-monthly --query "notifications"
```

In the portal, **Subscriptions** > lab subscription > **Budgets** > `bud-az104-monthly` shows the amount, the
reset period and the three alert conditions.

### Validation checklist

Tick each check only after you have seen the expected result yourself. The lab counts as **validated** when every box
here is ticked.

- [ ] `ag-az104-budget` shows **Enabled** (`True`) with your email address as a receiver.
- [ ] The action group test reported success, and the test email arrived in your inbox.
- [ ] `bud-az104-monthly` exists on the lab subscription with one **Forecasted** and two **Actual** alert conditions.
- [ ] Every alert condition lists `ag-az104-budget` as its action group.
- [ ] The saved cost analysis view `az104 lab costs` groups by the `az104-module` tag.

## What just happened?

You built an alarm in three parts: the **budget** compares cost with your ceiling, the **action group** carries the
message, and **cost analysis** tells you where the money went. You tested the message path on its own, because a
chain tested only end to end fails in a way you can't diagnose. Notice the limit too: cost data lags by hours and the
budget is evaluated daily, so the alarm tells you about spend that has already happened. The control that actually
stops spend is the teardown at the end of every lab. Budgets and alerts come back as an exam skill in
[01-03](../../content/01-identities-governance/01-03-subscriptions-and-governance.md), and action groups in
[05-01](../../content/05-monitor/05-01-monitor-resources.md).

## Teardown

**This lab is the exception: keep what it built.** `rg-az104-core`, the action group and the budget stay for the whole
course. Teardown here is a check that nothing else was left behind.

- [ ] `rg-az104-core` contains only `ag-az104-budget`: `Get-AzResource -ResourceGroupName 'rg-az104-core'`.
- [ ] No other resource group was created by this lab.
- [ ] Any role you assigned to your own account only for this lab has been removed.

Only when you finish the course, or abandon the lab subscription, remove them in this order: delete the budget on the
subscription's **Budgets** page (or `az consumption budget delete --budget-name bud-az104-monthly`), then delete the
resource group with `Remove-AzResourceGroup -Name rg-az104-core`, which removes the action group with it. The budget
isn't inside the resource group, so deleting the group alone leaves it behind.
