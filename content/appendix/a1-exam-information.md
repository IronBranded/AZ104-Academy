# Exam Information

> **Which outline this Academy follows.** Every lesson is written against the AZ-104
> **skills measured as of April 17, 2026**, captured from Microsoft's study guide on
> 2026-09-29. When Microsoft publishes a new version, the outline-drift workflow opens an
> issue and the affected modules are updated.

## Which version applies to you

Microsoft updates the **English** exam first. Localized versions are updated
approximately **eight weeks later**, and Microsoft notes that this schedule can slip.
If you are taking the exam in another language within about eight weeks of an outline
change, the previous outline may still apply to you. Check the study guide, which shows
both versions while a change is in progress.

If the exam isn't available in your preferred language, you can request an additional
30 minutes to complete it.

## What the outline means

- **The bullets illustrate, they don't fence.** Microsoft states that the bullets under
  each skill show how the skill is assessed, and that related topics may be covered.
  This Academy teaches the bullets and marks any adjacent material as
  *context, not a measured skill*.
- **GA first.** Most questions cover generally available features. Preview features can
  appear if they are commonly used. Lessons describe GA behaviour and label anything in
  preview.

Weights are published as ranges. This Academy shows them as ranges and never converts
them to a single number or a readiness percentage.

## Who the exam is for

Microsoft's audience profile describes an administrator who implements, manages and
monitors an Azure environment, and expects experience with PowerShell, the Azure CLI,
the Azure portal, ARM templates or Bicep files, and Microsoft Entra ID.

## Scoring, practice and renewal

- A score of **700 or greater** is required to pass.
- Microsoft offers a **free practice assessment** and an **exam sandbox** for exploring
  the exam interface. Links are in Sources.
- Associate certifications **expire annually**. You renew with a **free** online
  assessment on Microsoft Learn.
- If you need assistive devices, extra time or other changes to the exam experience,
  you can request an accommodation.

This page deliberately doesn't state exam length, question count or price: they
aren't on the study guide, and they change. Check the exam page when you schedule.
